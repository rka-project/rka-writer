# Convergence Protocol

The researcher works through epistemic, discourse and linguistic loops:
what can be claimed; what readers need in what order; and how the admitted
meaning should be expressed. Later insight can reopen an earlier commitment.

The [framework synthesis](../design/writer-framework-synthesis.md) consolidates
these loops. The operational refinements below remain proposals under ADR 0008,
not a new implementation gate or an evaluated workflow.

## A useful discussion, not a form sequence

Ask one consequential question with evidence and alternatives. Let the author
reason in natural language. At a commitment point, preview the exact coupled
changes and exclusions. One clear approval can cover that displayed bundle;
each generated sentence is still separately reviewed and accepted.

Keep storage, discussion, approval and generation granularities independent.
A decision scheduler recommends high-impact unresolved issues but allows the
author to choose focus. Do not require a fixed number of turns or options.

## Local readiness, global awareness

Paper question, claim portfolio, Paper Spine, section purpose and paragraph
allocation ground the current target. Unrelated later sections may stay
provisional. Realization sees a compact paper capsule plus detailed paragraph
intent sequence and approved evidence/terms/style.

Human scratch text is permitted. Agent manuscript prose is not generated before
admission. Meaning-changing human edits reopen the relevant contract through
reconciliation without deleting or overwriting the edits.

## Change protocol

1. Preserve historical versions and current document bytes.
2. Identify downstream candidates using exact edges and consumed contracts.
3. Distinguish known-invalid, needs-review, preference review and unaffected.
4. Block affected production for invalid or unknown semantic effects.
5. Show impact and support explicit revalidation, revision, branching or
   retirement. Never silently rewrite or rebind.

## What convergence means

Before a repair, identify the defect and what would count as resolving it.
Missing support returns to the scientific discussion; a missing warrant or
reader move returns to the discourse plan; concept conflation returns to term
selection. Only a same-meaning expression defect proceeds as local wording work.
Repeated unsuccessful polishing calls for that diagnosis, not another generic
rewrite or a universal maximum number of rounds.

Review the result against the stated defect and protected meaning. A correct,
readable passage can pass unchanged. Keep necessary qualifications, technical
repetition and useful signposting even when they look formulaic. Report a
restored omitted qualifier as a change to expressed meaning even when the
underlying approved claim remains unchanged.

A usable stable argument is the goal, not fewer edits or more locked objects.
Measure author comprehension, settled high-level meaning, accidental versus
justified reopening, hidden inconsistency, review effort and reader
understanding. A justified pivot is healthy research, not an error metric.

Style convergence is separate. A preferred tone update need not invalidate
science; a changed concept or scope does require review. An author's accepted
sentence edit does not silently alter their global profile.

A local target may settle when its required meaning/terms are approved,
material support and scope blockers are resolved, the reader can recover the
intended takeaway and the author accepts the expression. This is provisional
closure, not a ban on reopening when evidence or understanding changes.
