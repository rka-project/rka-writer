# Product Principles

1. **Researcher-owned meaning.** Models propose; researchers approve exact,
   visible commitments. Approval is not proof of scientific support.
2. **Question-led evidence work.** Review support, scope and counterevidence
   before committing the story; retrieval success is not entailment.
3. **Progressive, local commitment.** Move from paper question to expression
   without requiring every future section to be finalized.
4. **Different granularities.** Store small artifacts, discuss consequential
   questions, approve coupled displayed versions, generate one intent at a time.
5. **Paper-centered interaction.** Keep the paper and outline visible;
   conversation supports thinking and graph detail is available on demand.
6. **No agent manuscript prose before admission.** The author may write notes
   and edit directly; preserve those bytes and reconcile rather than overwrite.
7. **One sentence with context.** Use a compact version-bound paper capsule and
   full paragraph intent plan, not independent sentence prompts or a Core dump.
8. **Concepts before labels.** Technical correctness and field meaning outrank
   personal habits; lock needed terms before realization.
9. **Impact, never silent rewriting.** Classify changed dependencies, block
   invalid/uncertain affected production and keep historical versions intact.
10. **Separate lifecycle, readiness and assessment.** Accepted history,
    current dependencies and scientific support answer different questions.
11. **Honest guarantees.** Programs enforce declared structural constraints;
    semantic correctness and reader quality require separate assessment.
12. **Separated authority.** Core research truth, Writer authoring decisions and
    document bytes have distinct owners and explicit reconciliation.
13. **Advisory isolated review.** Requested whole-paper review is compatible;
    findings do not automatically become edits or accepted meaning.
14. **Consent-based style.** Sources suggest; authors endorse scoped term,
    prose and tone preferences. Accepting an edit does not train a global rule.
15. **Bounded source use.** Exclude whole samples from realization. Optional
    small approved examples need separate evaluation, provenance and copy review.
16. **Subscription-included-only.** Verify authentication, no-extra-paid usage
    and actual host isolation independently. Unknown properties fail closed.
17. **Local-first, honestly described.** Preserve local recovery/export; disclose
    selected content sent to cloud-backed hosts.
18. **Validate before freezing.** Observe author/reader behavior early. A
    documentation merge is not W0/W1 completion or host qualification.
