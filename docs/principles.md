# Product Principles

1. **Researcher-owned meaning.** A model may propose meaning, but only a
   researcher may approve consequential meaning.
2. **Question before retrieval.** Retrieval is scoped by the paper question;
   story commitment follows evidence review.
3. **No prose before semantic admission.** A sentence can be realized only
   from an admitted intent whose upstream dependencies are ready.
4. **One sentence, paragraph awareness.** Generation is bounded to one sentence
   while receiving the approved paragraph contract and relevant neighboring
   intents.
5. **Concepts before labels.** Concepts may remain provisional early; exact
   terminology is locked before realization.
6. **Exact dependencies.** Every artifact version names the upstream versions
   from which it was derived.
7. **Invalidate, never silently regenerate.** Upstream change marks affected
   artifacts stale and produces an impact report.
8. **Lifecycle is not readiness.** Stored state records what happened;
   computed readiness determines whether downstream work may proceed.
9. **Core and Writer authority remain separate.** Core owns research truth;
   Writer owns manuscript organization and expression.
10. **Review remains isolated and advisory.** Reviewer context never becomes a
    drafting persona or automatic mutation source.
11. **Prose is compiled output.** Authoring Graph state must remain useful even
    if manuscript prose is regenerated or edited.
12. **Local-first recovery.** Authoring state and manuscript bytes remain
    readable, exportable, and recoverable without a particular model provider.
