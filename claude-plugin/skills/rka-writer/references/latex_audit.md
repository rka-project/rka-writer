# LaTeX and rendered-page audit (optional)

Use this reference after content edits when the manuscript is maintained in
LaTeX. It is a practical inspection pass, not a writing-style gate.

1. Build from a clean state with the project's documented command.
2. Resolve compilation errors, missing citations, and broken references.
3. Inspect every rendered page rather than relying only on the log.
4. Check figure and table legibility at normal reading size.
5. Confirm captions explain what is shown and match the surrounding prose.
6. Check that floats appear near the discussion they support.
7. Look for clipped content, bad page breaks, orphan headings, excessive blank
   space, and unreadably small labels.
8. Verify page limits and formatting rules against the current official venue
   source.

When revising layout, preserve semantic emphasis. A central result may deserve
full width; secondary detail may move to an appendix. Do not shrink essential
text or figures merely to satisfy a page target before considering clearer
organization.
